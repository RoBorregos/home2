# Manipulation benchmark, November 2026

Real robot comparison of the geometric grasp generator and GPD, run on 2026-11-01 and 02 on the Orin with OMPL. Each trial ran the full stack with the on-robot detector, and an operator reported each outcome. These are the numbers reported in the 2027 Team Description Paper.

## Results

Objects held, without fallback between methods:

| Scenario | Geometric | GPD | Pick time per held object, failures included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alone, 7 objects at 5 positions | 23/35 (66%) | 12/35 (34%) | 14.4 s vs 33.6 s |
| Clutter, 5 scenes of 4 or 5 objects | 20/24 (83%) | 11/24 (46%) | 11.8 s vs 28.7 s |

McNemar exact test on the paired trials: p = 0.007 alone, p = 0.012 in clutter. Clutter positions were closer to the arm than the isolated ones, so the two rows are not comparable.

- The stack reported success in 24 of the 36 GPD failures: 22 objects not held, and 2 picks of the wrong object.
- All 67 held objects were placed correctly on a free surface. Spatial requests and pouring were not tested.
- Spoon with the flat strategy, not part of the comparison: 1/5.

## Files

- `experiment.csv`: every attempt, including the discarded ones.
- `experiment_notes.txt`: operator observations and protocol changes.
- `scenes.json`: clutter scenes, generated by `generate_scenes.py` with seed 2027. Only scenes 1 to 5 were run.

## Reading the CSV

`suite` decides whether a row counts:

| `suite` | Meaning | Counted |
|---|---|---|
| `object` | Isolated trial; `trial` 1 to 5 is the table position | Yes |
| `clutter` | Clutter trial in scene `scene` | Yes |
| `pilot` | Before the protocol was fixed, or with `TABLE_CLEARANCE` at 5 mm | No |
| `excluded_vision` | The 2D detector did not see the object | No |
| `invalid` | The setup was wrong, or `move_group` crashed during the attempt | No |
| `rerun` | Replaced by an operator-chosen retry | No |

`failure_source` is `stack` when the action failed and `operator` when the stack reported success but the operator saw a failure. `retained` is the operator verdict, and `pick_s` and `place_s` are the action durations.

`git_sha` is `8f05b936+exp` or `8f05b936+exp+clear0`: `main` at `8f05b936` plus the experiment changes, with `TABLE_CLEARANCE` at 5 mm and 0 mm respectively. GPD does not use `TABLE_CLEARANCE`.

## Known limits

- These runs do not show that 0 mm picks better. With the same 4 objects, geometric held 12/20 at 5 mm and 10/20 at 0 mm, with table rejections down from 5 to 2.
- `coca_cola_can` fell from 4/5 to 1/5 at 0 mm, and the operator saw it slip. The gripper was then checked and looked fine, so the cause is unknown.
- The banana GPD run was made before the first stack restart and was not repeated.
- 8 attempts were excluded for detector misses, and `coca_cola_can` was removed from scene 5 for both methods.
- `move_group` crashed 4 times (#1447). Attempts were repeated only when the crash invalidated them.
- Positions were set by hand, with tape marks and a reference image, so they are approximate.

## Reproducing

From `manipulation/packages/pick_and_place/pick_and_place/`, with `ppc.launch.py` running:

```bash
python3 generate_scenes.py
python3 scene_overlay.py --scene 1
python3 experiment_trial.py pick --method geometric --object apple
python3 experiment_trial.py place
```

`scene_overlay.py` serves the live camera blended with the saved reference on port 8090. `experiment_trial.py log` appends one row to the CSV.
