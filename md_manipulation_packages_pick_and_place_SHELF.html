Shelf pick and place

The robot measures the shelf in front of it with the eye-in-hand ZED2 (no per-arena heights), then picks from and places on its levels. A shelf pick or place without a scan from where the robot stands fails.

The flow and its files

Goal What manipulation_core does Result
SCAN_SHELF Visits the two survey poses, captures each view, detects the shelf, files what the camera sees under its level, adds the boxes to the scene and keeps the scan while the base stays put shelf_levels, each with objects
PICK, pick_params.from_shelf (optional shelf_level hint) Finds the object's level (the hint, then where the scan saw it, then every level), faces it, grasps, backs out shelf_level it picked from
PLACE, place_params.shelf_level = N Faces level N and places inside the scanned board

Manual test: keyboard_input.py -12 (scan), -11 (pick), -4 (place on a level).

Detection (<tt>perception_3d</tt>)

DetectShelf is hosted by grasp_generator, which owns the depth image, intrinsics and TF. It only detects: the boxes come back in the answer and only the core writes them to the scene.

  1. Frames captured at each survey pose accumulate in base_link.
  2. Crop, 1 cm voxels, normals. Up-facing horizontal points give one Z peak per board; peaks narrower than 60 % of the shelf or with gaps are objects. Down-facing peaks give the thickness and a board above the camera; vertical planes at the extremes are the walls.
  3. Boards and walls become boxes (frida_shelf_*), grown 2 cm into the free space except the board tops.

On-site tunables are grasp_generator parameters under shelf.: the search box (x_min, x_max, y_half, z_min, z_max), min_width_fraction, min_level_spacing, min_level_points.

Survey pose Joints (deg) Camera in base_link Sees
shelf_survey_high -90, -45, -90, 0, 20, 45 (0.19, 0.06, 1.40), 23 deg down upper boards, a board above the camera
shelf_survey_low -90, -105, 0, 0, 15, 45 (0.21, 0.06, 0.95), 48 deg down the low compartments

Pick: the <tt>shelf</tt> strategy

classification.py sends every shelf pick to the shelf profile:

  • Cluster. The object is segmented inside its compartment (compartment_band): the default band merged it with the boards of a closed cabinet.
  • Frontal grasp (shelf_frontal_grasps). Fingertips just past the object's middle, at mid height, clear of the board and the ceiling; fingers close sideways, the ZED points up. Tilted 10, 0, 20 deg down, and 30, 45 deg with nothing above.
  • Approach. Pre-grasp, then straight in by xArm cartesian velocity (mode 5): no collision check against the shelf boxes. Then GPD if no frontal grasp works.
  • Exit. Two straight MoveIt paths, collision checked: 2 cm up, 15 cm back. When cut, the return to table_stare is planned around the boxes.

Place

  • The held object's centre stays inside the scanned board (8 cm from the front, away from the back and sides), and keeps the "up" it had when picked.
  • When a rung fails, the next is 2 cm higher; nothing is released short of it.

Tuning

Every shelf number is in config/pick_profiles.yaml → shelf.shelf: the frontal grasp, the exit, facing a level, the place margins and how far the base may move (3 cm / 3 deg) before the scan is dropped. manipulation_core parameters:

ros2 param set /manipulation_core shelf_acceleration 0.3 # default 0.5
ros2 param set /manipulation_core shelf_place_straight true # default false

Known limits

  • An object under ~12 cm (an apple) gets no frontal grasp and GPD rarely reaches it.
  • The place puts link_eef 10 cm above the board whatever the grasp height.
  • The top shelf (~1.30 m) is at the edge of the arm's reach for a place.
  • A level's objects are what the survey poses see; a pick then searches the others.
